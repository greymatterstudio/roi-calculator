    How this is worked out

    • Views each month are videos published times average views per video. Older videos keep getting watched, so each later month adds the monthly build on top of month one.
    • A lead is a viewer who opts in with name, email and phone. Leads are followed up by email, text and phone until they book.
    • Booked calls times show-up rate times close rate gives new clients. New clients times your offer price gives revenue.
    • Investment is charged at the start of each 90-day block. Revenue is counted in the month the views happen, so real cash will arrive a little later.

    This is a projection built from the numbers you enter. It is an estimate, and actual results depend on your offer, your market and your sales team.
